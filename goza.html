Terms & Policies

1. Introduction

Welcome to GoZA. By accessing or using our application, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. Please read them carefully.

2. Service Description

GoZA provides a platform connecting riders with independent drivers for on-demand transportation. We act as a technology intermediary, not a direct transport operator.

3. User Responsibilities

You must provide accurate pickup and destination details. You must ensure you are reachable on your provided mobile number during a trip, and behave respectfully toward drivers.

4. Payments and Refunds

Fares are estimated upfront based on distance and ride type. Actual charges may vary slightly in the event of major route diversions. Refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by our support team.

5. Liability and Insurance

While we strive for completely safe rides, GoZA's liability is limited. Drivers are expected to maintain valid licensing, vehicle insurance, and roadworthy vehicles.

6. Privacy Policy & POPIA

We process your personal information strictly in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013. Your data (name, location, phone number) is used only to facilitate trips, verify driver identity, and improve your app experience.
